EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version: 2026.1

Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year.
Employees should request planned leave through the company leave portal at least five working days before the intended start date whenever practical.
Unused annual leave may be carried forward according to the applicable local employment policy.

Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role and team operating model support remote work.
Employees working remotely must remain reachable during their agreed working hours and must use company-approved devices and collaboration tools.
Managers may require employees to attend on-site meetings when business needs require physical presence.

Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday, excluding approved holidays.
Teams may have different schedules when documented by the manager.

Information Security
Company information must only be stored in approved systems. Employees must not upload confidential company information to personal cloud storage.
